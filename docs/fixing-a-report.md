# Fixing a report after it's been sent

Sometimes a report goes out with something wrong on it — a misspelled name, a
typo in a note, an item marked Good that should have been Attention. You can
correct it yourself and send out a fresh copy. **The customer's link doesn't
change**, so you don't need to email them a new one unless you want to.

## How to do it

1. Open the job ticket and click the **Office review** link.
2. Type the office code. You'll only be asked once per day, or until you close
   your browser.
3. Fix whatever's wrong. Changed boxes turn blue so you can see what you've
   touched.
4. Put your name in the box at the bottom.
5. Click **Regenerate report**.

That's it. The link the customer already has now opens the corrected report.

## What you can and can't change

**You can change:** the customer's name, email, phone and address; the
inspection date; any note; any Good / Monitor / Attention rating; and the
caption under any photo.

Some rows are a straight Yes/No question rather than a rating — things like
"Algae Presence" or "GFCI Outlets". Those give you a Yes/No box instead, which
is what the tech answered and what the report prints. The Good or Attention
behind it follows automatically from your answer.

**You can't change:** the chemistry readings, the photos themselves, or the
inspector's name and certification. Chemistry numbers are measurements the tech
took at the pool — if one is wrong, the pool needs re-testing, not retyping.

If you change a rating, the summary at the top of the report and the counts
underneath it update to match automatically. You don't have to fix those
yourself.

## Two things it does NOT do

**It does not update the customer's details in HubSpot.** If you correct the
name, email, phone, address, city or ZIP, the report is fixed but the contact
record still has the old version. Go and fix it there too. The screen will
remind you and tell you exactly which ones you changed.

**It does not update the summary text on the job ticket.** If you change a
rating, the wording written into the ticket when the job was filed still
describes the original findings. That's expected — the report behind the link
is the current one, and the ticket text is a snapshot of what the tech
originally submitted.

Nothing is re-sent to the customer automatically. Regenerating a report does not
create a second ticket or a second job.

## The change history

Every edit is recorded at the bottom of the page: what was changed, from what to
what, by whom, and when. Newest first.

This is for the office only. It never appears on the customer's report and it
can't be edited or deleted — including by you, right after you make a change.
If you change something back, that's two entries in the history, not zero. The
tech's original finding always stands on the record.

The customer's copy shows only a small "Revised" line with the date, which is
why it's worth getting the fix right rather than making six passes at it.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Someone else saved changes to this report."** Two people had the report
  open at once and the other person saved first. Reload the page and make your
  changes again — nothing of yours was saved, and nothing of theirs was lost.
- **"That code isn't right."** Check the office code. The same message appears
  for a broken link, so it's worth checking both.
- **"The record of this change didn't save."** Rare, and it means the opposite
  of the others: the customer's link IS showing your corrected report, but the
  office copy of what you changed didn't record. Don't redo the edit — tell
  whoever manages the site.
- **Anything else.** If a message says nothing was changed, then nothing was —
  the old report is still on the customer's link, untouched.

Previous versions of a report are kept. Nothing you do here destroys a copy that
a customer might already have in their inbox.
